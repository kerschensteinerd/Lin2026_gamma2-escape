# Figure Sx. Sensitivity of modeled calcium recruitment and spatial spread to the voltage-to-calcium transformation

**Provisional numbering; related to Figure 4.** Local calcium responses were recomputed from archived voltage responses for 100-µm OFF spots and the original focal-input protocol. Activation midpoint and slope were varied over the prior calibration bounds, from -45 to -20 mV in 0.5-mV steps and 1 to 6 mV in 0.1-mV steps (2,601 parameter pairs). Every control/reduced-inhibition pair used the same activation curve, fixed driving factor, and 50-ms clearance. Electrical responses, geometry, excitation, inhibitory conductance, and input locations were held fixed. The reduced condition retained 25% of control effective inhibitory conductance. Calcium conversion preceded averaging over the same 75 OFF input contacts on one reconstruction; these sites and parameter combinations are not independent biological replicates.

**A.** Mean calcium AUC across contrast for the exact selected parameters (midpoint -37.47892660844856 mV; slope 1.3984142481316566 mV). Both conditions are shown with solid lines. Control is gray and reduced inhibition is orange throughout. Symbols identify the 15%, 30%, and 70% contrasts used for the primary comparison. AUC is integrated over the 3-s stimulus and expressed in arbitrary influx units multiplied by ms²; plotted values are divided by 1,000. It is a model proxy, not calibrated calcium concentration or fluorescence.

**B.** Absolute responses at the domain corner (-20 mV, 6 mV), illustrating a reversal of preferential fractional enhancement. The effect remains an absolute response increase, but gain at 15% and 30% is smaller than gain at 70%. Gain(C) = AUC_reduced(C)/AUC_control(C); P15 = gain(15%)/gain(70%) and P30 = gain(30%)/gain(70%). Here P15 = 0.719 and P30 = 0.866. The y-axis range differs from A and is explicitly labeled. This corner was included in the prespecified domain; its role as a counterexample was identified after examining the completed sweep.

**C, D.** Full-grid maps of log2 P15 and log2 P30. Positive values indicate preferential fractional enhancement at the lower contrast; negative values indicate the reverse. Black contours mark zero, equivalent to P = 1. At the selected parameters, P15 = 11.362 and P30 = 7.215. Both preferences exceeded 1 at 1,561 of 2,525 technically evaluable grid pairs. These counts describe coverage of the specified uniform coordinate grid, not a biological probability or confidence level. They do not imply that all tested activation curves are physiologically plausible.

**E.** Reduced-minus-control difference in the separately endpoint-normalized 30% response, S(C) = [AUC(C) - AUC(15%)]/[AUC(70%) - AUC(15%)]. Each condition is normalized to its own endpoints; this does not establish equal absolute responses. Black contour, zero separation. The separation was positive at 1,966 of 2,594 evaluable pairs, and ranged from -0.0638 to 0.8892. Its sign can therefore reverse even though absolute AUC increases.

**F.** Change in the first calcium 1/e crossing after inhibition reduction at the original focal input site (compartment 4705), with identical focal conductance in both conditions. Calcium profiles were normalized to their respective source amplitudes, then summarized in cable-length-weighted 5-µm path-distance bins. First crossings were linearly interpolated between adjacent bins; they are not fitted exponential length constants. All 2,574 parameter pairs with crossings observed in both conditions broadened after inhibition reduction (reduced/control crossing ratio 1.261-9.673). For 27 pairs, no reduced-condition crossing was observed within the sampled arbor; those values are censored and shown in gray, not assigned zero or the maximum path length. This panel tests the calcium transformation at the original source, not robustness across input locations.

**G.** Source-normalized focal calcium profiles for the selected transformation, shown with solid lines using the same colors as A. The dotted horizontal line marks 1/e. Control/reduced first crossings were 18.78/34.34 µm. Voltage spread is unchanged by this postprocessing sweep.

**H.** Calcium-only horizontal contrast equivalence. At each parameter pair, the full control calcium-AUC curve was inverted by PCHIP without extrapolation. Each nonzero reduced-condition sampled response was mapped to an equivalent control contrast only when technically usable and within the simulated control response range. The map shows the arithmetic mean of valid equivalent-control/reduced-contrast ratios. Means ranged from 3.115 to 3.486 across 2,590 evaluable pairs; the selected pair gave 3.2685 from four matches. Dotted contours separate adjacent valid-match counts (0-4); the supported contrast points vary across the grid. These descriptive means and ranges are not uncertainty intervals or a fitted common biological gain. This calcium-only metric is distinct from the manuscript's combined voltage/calcium marker.

Stars mark the exact selected pair, evaluated separately from the regular grid. Open squares mark the domain corner in B. The entire prescribed domain is shown. Gray in C-E denotes unavailable metrics under the prespecified small-denominator safeguard: a control denominator or normalization span at or below 0.15 arbitrary influx units × ms². In F, gray denotes the censored crossings described above; in H it denotes an unavailable mean horizontal equivalence. No parameters were refitted and no effects were excluded because they contradicted the proposed mechanism. Full raw responses, plotted arrays, technical masks, individual contrast matches, and source hashes accompany the figure.

## Companion page: retained-inhibition sensitivity

**Figure Sx - companion. Dependence of the calcium sweep on retained inhibitory conductance.** Rows show 20% (A-C), 25% (D-F), and 30% (G-I) retained effective inhibition. Columns show log2 P15, log2 P30, and the normalized 30% shape difference, using the definitions, markers, masks, and zero contours of Figure Sx. Color scales are identical within each column and match the corresponding main panels. Both fractional preferences exceeded 1 at 1,692, 1,561, and 1,457 of 2,525 evaluable grid pairs for 20%, 25%, and 30% retained inhibition, respectively. All conditions use the same transformation at a given grid point; calcium parameters were not recalibrated for each retained fraction. The page shows sensitivity of the archived electrical conditions to the calcium transformation, not a sweep of membrane parameters or inhibitory spatial layouts.
